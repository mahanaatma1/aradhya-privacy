Aradhya privacy policy

Applies to the Android app com.aradhya.in. Last updated 24 November 2026.

Aradhya does not collect your personal data. The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and its Android release does not have permission to use the internet.

What stays on your phone

  • Your name and chosen deity, if you enter them
  • Journal entries, bookmarks, notes, japa counts, habits and reading progress
  • Temple visits and notes
  • Birth details you enter for kundli and matching, including those of another person

We cannot see this information. The app is set not to take part in Android cloud backup.

Location

Location is optional. If you allow it, it is read while you use the app to work out panchang timings and nearby temples. It is not sent to us or anyone else. Without it, the app uses a default city.

Notifications and alarms

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.

Sharing and links

When you share a card, or open a map, website, phone number or temple link, Android passes it to the app you choose. That app's own privacy policy then applies. Text to speech uses the voice engine installed on your phone.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall it. There is no copy for us to delete or restore.

Children

The app is meant for people aged 13 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will appear on this page.
